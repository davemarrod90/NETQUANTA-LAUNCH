What NetQuanta does

Two things make up most engagements. The rest are available on their own or as part of a larger project.

Network audit

A full read of what you actually have running, where the risk sits, and what it would take to fix. Most engagements start here.

  • Topology and traffic-path review
  • Single points of failure, mapped
  • Firmware, licensing, and end-of-life exposure
  • Written findings with priorities and cost

Design and deployment

New sites, office moves, or replacing a network that grew by accident. Built with the redundancy and segmentation an ISP would expect.

  • Multi-site WAN and VLAN design
  • Hardware selection without vendor bias
  • Configuration, staging, and cutover
  • Documentation you keep

Carrier infrastructure

MPLS, BGP peering, and carrier handoffs, scaled down to what a business actually needs.

Security and segmentation

Firewall policy, ACLs, and separating guest, staff, and payment traffic properly.

Troubleshooting

Intermittent drops, slow links, routing that misbehaves after a change. Root cause, not guesswork.

Ongoing support

Monitoring, config changes, and a number to call when something breaks.
